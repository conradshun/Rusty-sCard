### **Key Features:**

**For Students:**

- **Create & Upload Flashcard Sets** - Add author names, descriptions, and tags to organized sets
- **Bulk Import** - Upload `.txt` files with semicolon-separated Q&A pairs for quick card creation
- **Interactive Study Modes** - Click-to-flip flashcard interface with progress tracking and card shuffling
- **Browse & Search** - Discover flashcard sets and filter by tags across the community
- **Edit Sets** - Modify existing sets, add/remove cards, and support both text and image content


**For Admin:**

- **Admin Dashboard** - Password-protected access to manage the entire platform
- **User Analytics** - Track how many unique students are using the app
- **Content Management** - Delete flashcard sets, individual cards, or tags as needed
- **Full Platform Oversight** - Monitor and maintain community study materials


### **Core Purpose:**

Rusty's Card simplifies collaborative learning by allowing students to easily create, share, and reuse study materials in one centralized location without needing individual user accounts.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (PostgreSQL), shadcn/ui
